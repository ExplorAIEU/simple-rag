# 产品目录 / Product Catalog

## VisionInspect

VisionInspect 是用于生产线质量检查的计算机视觉平台。它可以识别划痕、缺件和装配错误，并与现有相机及制造执行系统集成。基础许可证每条生产线每年 120,000 元。

VisionInspect is a computer-vision platform for production-line quality inspection. It detects scratches, missing components, and assembly errors, and integrates with existing cameras and manufacturing execution systems. The base license costs RMB 120,000 per production line per year.

## PredictFlow

PredictFlow 使用设备传感器数据预测维护需求。标准版支持最多 100 台设备，包括异常警报和维护仪表板，每年价格为 80,000 元。

PredictFlow uses equipment sensor data to predict maintenance needs. The Standard plan supports up to 100 machines and includes anomaly alerts and a maintenance dashboard. It costs RMB 80,000 per year.

## KnowledgeBox

KnowledgeBox 是完全在客户网络中运行的本地文档问答系统。它支持中文和英文的 PDF、Word 及文本文件。数据不会发送到公共云。企业版起价为每年 150,000 元，包括部署和两天的现场培训。

KnowledgeBox is an on-premises document question-answering system that runs entirely inside the customer's network. It supports Chinese and English PDF, Word, and text files. Data is not sent to a public cloud. Enterprise pricing starts at RMB 150,000 per year and includes deployment and two days of on-site training.

## 支持服务 / Support

所有产品均包含工作日 9:00 至 18:00 的电子邮件支持，目标响应时间为四小时。高级支持额外收费，可提供全天候严重故障支持和一小时响应目标。

All products include email support from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on working days, with a four-hour response target. Premium support is available at additional cost and provides 24/7 critical-incident coverage with a one-hour response target.
