# 差旅与报销政策 / Travel and Expense Policy

## 审批 / Approval

所有差旅必须在预订前获得直属经理批准。预计总费用超过 8,000 元人民币的行程还需要部门负责人批准。国际差旅需要首席运营官批准。

All business travel must be approved by the employee's direct manager before booking. Trips expected to cost more than RMB 8,000 also require department-head approval. International travel requires approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

## 交通 / Transportation

员工应选择合理且经济的交通方式。飞行时间少于六小时应预订经济舱；超过六小时可以预订高级经济舱。高铁行程可以预订二等座。

Employees should choose reasonable and economical transportation. Economy class is required for flights under six hours; premium economy is permitted for longer flights. Second class may be booked for high-speed rail travel.

## 酒店与餐饮 / Hotels and Meals

酒店每晚报销上限为一线城市 900 元、其他城市 600 元。出差期间的餐饮补贴为每天 200 元。客户招待不计入每日餐饮补贴，但必须注明客户姓名和业务目的。

The nightly hotel limit is RMB 900 in tier-one cities and RMB 600 in other cities. The meal allowance is RMB 200 per day. Client entertainment is separate from the daily allowance, but the client name and business purpose must be recorded.

## 报销时间 / Reimbursement Deadline

员工应在行程结束后的十个工作日内提交报销申请，并附上发票或电子收据。财务部通常会在申请获批后的五个工作日内付款。

Employees should submit expenses with invoices or electronic receipts within ten working days after a trip. Finance normally pays approved claims within five working days.
