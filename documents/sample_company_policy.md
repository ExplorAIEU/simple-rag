# 示例公司员工手册 / Sample Company Handbook

## 工作时间 / Working Hours

示例公司的标准工作时间为周一至周五上午 9:00 至下午 5:30。员工每天有一小时午休时间。经直属经理批准，员工可以每周在家办公两天。

Sample Company's standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Employees receive a one-hour lunch break. With approval from their direct manager, employees may work from home two days per week.

## 年假 / Annual Leave

全职员工每个日历年享有 15 天带薪年假。入职第一年的年假按剩余月份比例计算。员工应至少提前五个工作日提交休假申请。

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is prorated during the first year of employment. Employees should submit leave requests at least five working days in advance.

## 培训预算 / Learning Budget

每位员工每年可申请最高 5,000 元人民币的职业培训预算。该预算可以用于课程、书籍和行业认证，但需要部门负责人事先批准。

Each employee may request up to RMB 5,000 per year for professional development. The budget can cover courses, books, and industry certifications, subject to advance approval by the department head.

## 信息安全 / Information Security

公司机密资料不得上传到未经批准的公共云服务或公共 AI 工具。员工应使用公司批准的本地系统处理敏感文件，并立即向 IT 团队报告疑似数据泄露事件。

Confidential company material must not be uploaded to unapproved public cloud services or public AI tools. Employees should use company-approved local systems for sensitive files and report suspected data leaks to the IT team immediately.
