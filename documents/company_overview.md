# 示例公司简介 / Sample Company Overview

## 公司概况 / Company Profile

示例智能科技有限公司成立于 2018 年，总部位于深圳，并在上海和成都设有办公室。截至 2026 年 9 月，公司共有 240 名员工。

Sample Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 2018. Its headquarters are in Shenzhen, with additional offices in Shanghai and Chengdu. As of September 2026, the company has 240 employees.

## 使命 / Mission

公司的使命是通过安全、实用的人工智能工具，帮助制造企业提高效率和产品质量。公司重点服务消费电子、汽车零部件和工业设备行业。

The company's mission is to help manufacturers improve efficiency and product quality through secure, practical AI tools. Its primary industries are consumer electronics, automotive components, and industrial equipment.

## 2026 年目标 / 2026 Goals

公司 2026 年的主要目标包括：将年度经常性收入提高 30%，把客户续约率保持在 92% 以上，并将平均项目交付周期从 12 周缩短到 9 周。

The main goals for 2026 are to increase annual recurring revenue by 30%, maintain customer renewal above 92%, and reduce average project delivery time from 12 weeks to 9 weeks.
